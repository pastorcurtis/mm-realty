The Mission

We design, build, restore, and manage housing for veterans, seniors, and working families to a standard the luxury market would recognize — financed through partnerships that prove this work is the most disciplined deal flow in American real estate.

M&M Realty is the Delaware-based developer building the housing America has structurally underserved — veterans communities, 55+ residences, and affordable family homes — to investment-grade standards. Unlike firms that treat these segments as ESG line items or community-development concessions, M&M underwrites them as primary thesis: the most disciplined, most enduring, most defensible deal flow in the asset class.
