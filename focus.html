Model Project

Renovate, Hold, and Build

Our underwriting model pairs two strategies on one site: restore an existing building as rentals, and build new cottages for 55+ and veteran buyers. Cottage sales pay down the construction loan; the rentals carry what remains.

8 Renovated Rentals

An existing building restored in two phases. Target rent of $1,550, about 10% below the Delaware average of $1,732.

4 New Cottages

Built for sale to 55+ and veteran buyers at $350,000, below the Delaware median of $401,000.

$2.5M Total Cost

Financed at 75% loan-to-cost. Cottage sales retire most of the loan, leaving a small permanent loan on the rentals.
